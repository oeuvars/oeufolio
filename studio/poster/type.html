human
experience
I am happiest early,
in the quiet
hour before
dawn, with
a thing half
made on my
desk, turning
it over and over until
it gives, and I think a
life is mostly days like
that, slow ones, and
I would not trade a
single one of them.
at Ekarth i learned to speak for
what i had made, and ever since
i have wanted to make only the
kind of thing i would be glad
to stand beside, things that will
hold long after i have stopped
holding them.
at Cybership my days are parcels and printers and
the long quiet of warehouses at night, and when one
of them stops i go to it, whatever the hour, and stay
until it moves again, and then i sit a while in the
dark with the screen still lit and listen to it breathe,
and by morning the trucks are out as if nothing
happened at all. work passes like this.
ProperSKU was where it began, my first work,
learning the whole of it by hand, and i still learn that
way, slowly, by breaking things and sitting with them, and
somewhere along the way the work stopped being a job and
became the way i look at everything, a door, a chair, a song
on the way home, and some nights it all comes at once and i
feel, it is enough! it is enough!
