experience
I know now that for
one parcel to find
its door, I would
stay up all night.
I have watched the
warehouses go still
and stayed with them,
at Cybership, until
they moved again,
and by morning the
trucks were leaving
the loading docks as
if nothing had ever
happened at all.
something that explains why
Ekarth stays with me, a place
for money to meet the houses
not yet built, and the rooms
in four countries where i
stood and asked strangers
to believe in it with me.
a parcel is being weighed, a label is printing,
a truck is backing into the dock, a packer is
waiting on a box, an order is leaving for a door
in a town no one here has seen, a scanner beeps,
someone is calling about a shelf, a pallet comes
in wrapped in plastic, the night shift sweeps the
floor. work passes like this.
the first was ProperSKU, and i knew so little, and
the screen glowed a little more brightly each night
as the work came in, in waves, one client and then
another, which curved and swelled and broke upon
my desk, and i learned to swim in them, and something
opened in me, and waves of pure delight raced over
the floor of my mind, and i felt, before i knew it,
this is it! this is it!
