I do my best work
in the dark
before dawn,
a thing half
built on the
desk, and I
stay with it
until it gives. I have
come to think a life is
mostly days like that,
and I would not trade
a single one of them.
i learned to stand up and answer
for what i had built. since my
days at Nahar OM i have only
built what i would put my name
on, things that will hold long
after i have walked away.
my days are parcels, printers and the long quiet of
warehouses at night. when one of them stops i go
to it, whatever the hour, and i stay until it runs
again. at Cybership the trucks are out by morning
as if nothing ever happened, and that is how i like it.
work passes like this.
it all started with learning the whole of it by
hand. i still learn that way, by breaking things and sitting
with them until they make sense, and somewhere along the
way, back at ProperSKU, the work stopped being a job and
became the way i see everything. some nights it all comes
together, and i close the laptop and sit a while in the dark,
and for that night it is enough.
