human
experience
I have come to think
that work is
mostly the
hour before
anyone is
awake, with
a thing half
made on the desk, one
problem turned over
until it gives, and
that a life is days
like that, unseen,
and I would not trade
a single one.
at Ekarth i built a lending
platform and learned to pitch.
and i think now the work is
never only the code, it is the
people in the room, and whether
they walk out believing you a
little more than they did.
at Cybership a parcel is weighed, a label prints,
a truck backs into the dock, a packer waits on a
box, an order leaves for a town i will never see,
a warehouse goes quiet at midnight and my phone
lights up, a fix goes out before the night shift
sweeps the floor, the first truck is loaded in the
dark. work passes like this.
first came ProperSKU and websites for
clients, the whole stack learned by doing it, and
i still learn that way, by breaking things and
sitting with them, and somewhere along the way the
work stopped being a job and became the way i look
at everything, at a door, a chair, a song, and some
nights it all comes at once, and i feel, it is
enough! it is enough!
