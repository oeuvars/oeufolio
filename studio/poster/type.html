I do my best work
in the dark
before dawn,
a thing half
built on the
desk, and I
stay with it
until it gives. I have
come to think a life is
mostly days like that,
and I would not trade
a single one of them.
at Ekarth i learned to stand up
and answer for what i had built.
since then i have only built what
i am willing to put my name on,
things that will hold long after i
have walked away from them.
at Cybership my days are parcels, printers and the
long quiet of warehouses at night. when one of them
stops i go to it, whatever the hour, and i stay until
it runs again. by morning the trucks are out as if
nothing ever happened, and that is how i like it.
work passes like this.
ProperSKU was where it started, learning the
whole of it by hand. i still learn that way, by breaking things
and sitting with them until they make sense, and somewhere
along the way the work stopped being a job and became the
way i see everything. some nights it all comes together, and
i close the laptop and sit a while in the dark, and for that
night it is enough.
